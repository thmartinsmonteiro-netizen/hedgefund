## 3. This week's lens — {lens name}

Why this pocket, in two sentences.

### Candidate: {Name} ({TICKER:EXCHANGE})

**The business.** Two sentences, plain language.

**Why it's under-covered.** Which tests it passes, with cited evidence.

**The mispricing hypothesis.** What does the market appear to believe, and why might that be
wrong? If this section is weak, the idea should not be here.

**What would have to be true.** The two or three things that must hold for this to work.

**Bear case.** At least as long as the bull case. What kills it, who is on the other side of
the trade, and what they know.

**Numbers.** Each with a source and a date. Anything unverified marked `[UNVERIFIED]`.

*Repeat for 3–5 candidates maximum.*

| Ticker:Exch | Action | Size (%) | Size ({ccy}) | Limit | Thesis (one sentence) | Kill criteria | Review |

section is empty, the research was not thorough enough to trust.
