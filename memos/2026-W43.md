## 3. Holdings check: EYDAP (EYDAP:ATSE)
**Catalyst:** H1 2026 results, due by 30 September. Happened: published 24 September 2026 (Quartr summary of the company release, https://quartr.com/events/athens-water-supply-and-sewerage-company-sa-eydap-h1-2026-q-a_F1uW3eqA). Figures per that summary, which flags itself as machine-generated and should be checked against the ATHEX filing: revenue EUR 203.2m (+17.3% y/y), EBITDA EUR 42.6m (20.9% margin), net profit EUR 17.1m, operating cash flow EUR 25.2m, free cash flow EUR 5.0m, cash EUR 280.4m, equity EUR 823.3m.

**Kill criteria.** (a) H1 revenue below EUR 200m: not tripped, by EUR 3.2m. (b) Pricing reopened by RAAEY or ministry: no evidence found; a July draft water-sector law and a planned merger of 43 small utilities are open items, not reopenings. (c) Net debt before FY27: cash EUR 280m, debt figure not in the summary [UNVERIFIED]; not tripped on available evidence.

**Thesis.** The W37 memo underwrote run-rate EBITDA of about EUR 150m (adjusted 2025 EBITDA EUR 78.6m plus most of a EUR 78.3m annual revenue uplift quoted by management). H1 2026 EBITDA annualises to about EUR 85m. Revenue rose about EUR 30m in the half, so the uplift being recognised is roughly EUR 60m a year, and opex absorbed much of it. The mechanism worked (first clean half under the cap, profit instead of loss) but at about half the size I wrote down. Lesson for literature.md and screens.md: regulatory revenue is underwritten from the regulator's decision, not from a results briefing.

**Price.** EUR 13.94 bid on the Stuttgart listing, 21 Oct 2026 (Boerse Stuttgart, https://www.boerse-stuttgart.de/en/products/equities/stuttgart/931894-athens-water-and-sewerage-company-sa-eydap/). ATSE close not available in the feed today; the backfill will supply it and CHANGELOG.md records the substitution. From the EUR 12.02 entry that is about +16% in EUR; EURUSD fell from 1.164 to 1.123, so about +12% in USD before costs.

**Revised own value, arithmetic shown.** Run-rate EBITDA EUR 90m (H1 annualised, allowing some deferred regulatory revenue to arrive in H2). The multiple is 9x EV/EBITDA as in W37; it is an assumption, not a peer set [UNVERIFIED]. EV = 9 x 90 = EUR 810m. Equity = EV + cash (EUR 280m) with debt taken as zero = EUR 1,090m. Shares 106.5m [UNVERIFIED] gives EUR 10.2 per share; at EUR 100m EBITDA, EUR 11.1. Either way below EUR 13.94. The market is paying for something my model does not contain: the 43-utility consolidation, the EUR 2.8bn plan, or simply a re-rating of Greek regulated assets. I cannot underwrite any of those from the documents I have read.

**Verdict: exit in full at the 21 Oct close.** The event happened, the position is above my own fair value on the evidence I can defend, and the catalyst date has passed. Holding would be holding on a story. Booked in trades.csv and closed.csv.

**Bear case against exiting.** The revenue cap runs to 2029 and the H1 number may understate the run rate if regulatory revenue is back-end loaded; if full-year EBITDA lands near EUR 120m the stock is cheap at 13.94. That is the case for keeping it on the watchlist with a re-entry trigger (FY2026 results, late April 2027), not for holding without a date.
