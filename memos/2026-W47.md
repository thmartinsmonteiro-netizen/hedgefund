### Anti-thesis 1: Sanepar (SAPR11:B3), watchlist since W37

**The thesis as logged.** Court reversal of AGEPAR's decision to send 100% of a R$3.94bn precatório to users, or a privatisation signal after the October state election. Logged without a date, which the screen already said was its weakness.

**The case for dropping.**
1. *The administrative route is closed and the company has booked the loss.* AGEPAR approved Nota Técnica 002/2026 on 23 June 2026 (100% to modicity, split R$1.972bn bill discounts and R$1.972bn non-remunerated investment) and in August rejected Sanepar's administrative appeal, granting only 30 extra days to implement a 25% discount on the minimum tariff (Saneamento Pro, 20 Aug 2026). Sanepar's 2T26 shows a loss of about R$505m from "regulatory obligations" and an IRPJ dispute, and 9M26 net income is R$150.1m against R$1.72bn a year earlier, -91.3% (Gazeta do Paraná, 15 Nov 2026). When a company provisions the liability itself, the court case is an option on a refund, not the base case.
2. *Still no date.* The mandado de segurança filed in April has no reported hearing or judgment date; nothing on the IR site's publication list for 16 and 17 Nov mentions it (ri.sanepar.com.br, retrieved 18 Nov 2026). The screen requires a date. Nine weeks later there is none.
3. *The political catalyst arrived and said nothing.* Sergio Moro (PL) was elected governor in the first round on 4 Oct 2026 with 50.10% (Brasil de Fato, 4 Oct 2026). In his only detailed Sanepar interview before the vote he promised a "choque de gestão" and said he has no prejudice against privatisation but would only analyse it after the management overhaul, with no timetable (Tribuna PR, 8 Aug 2026). He takes office 1 Jan 2027. "No prejudice" is not a fato relevante.
4. *The price moved for the wrong reason.* SAPR11 fell 18.5% on 26 Oct alone (41.92 to 34.17) and is 34.16 on 17 Nov; in USD it is down 28.8% since 23 Oct and 14.0% since 9 Sep (prices.csv). A cheaper price with no event is a value idea, and the screen says "cheap is not an event".
5. *Factor.* A state-controlled Paraná utility whose regulator and whose controller both just changed, priced in a currency that fell 12.7% in four weeks, is the Brazil exposure the household already carries.

**The case for keeping it, so the verdict is honest.** 3T26 was operationally strong: net revenue R$1.94bn (+7.6%), EBITDA R$732.4m (+31.8%, margin 37.7% vs 30.8%), net income R$302.6m (+22.8%), net debt R$2.42bn and net debt/EBITDA 0.8x (Gazeta do Paraná, 15 Nov 2026). AGEPAR opened a public consultation on tariff-readjustment methodology in October 2026 (same source); a methodology decision would be a dated regulatory event and belongs to lens 5. A Moro government could also produce a follow-on or privatisation timetable in H1 2027. Those are reasons to re-screen under lens 5, not to hold a watchlist entry with no date.

**Base rate.** Lens 5 has no literature row; research/literature.md says so and this memo repeats it: no base rate available.

**Verdict: drop from the watchlist.** Logged in rejected.csv with three reconsider triggers: a scheduled judgment date on the mandado de segurança; a fato relevante from the state or company with a privatisation or follow-on timetable; or AGEPAR's methodology decision date. The feed keeps marking SAPR11 so the drop can be scored later.

### Anti-thesis 2: Light S.A. (LIGT3:B3), gated since W37

**The thesis as logged.** RJ closure plus capitalisation would make this the cleanest lens-1-meets-lens-5 situation on B3, if emergence leverage came in below 2.5x.

**What happened.** The capital increase was homologated on 15 July 2026: R$1.5bn, 238,473,768 new shares at R$6.29 each, total shares 611,029,092 after the issue, share capital R$6.97bn; each subscribed share came with two subscription bonuses exercisable at R$0.01 (476,947,536 bonuses, window 16 Jul to 14 Aug 2026), with lock-ups released 15% in Jan 2027, Jul 2027, Jan 2028, Jul 2028 and 40% in Jan 2029; the October 2024 mandatory convertible debentures met their conversion condition; the request to close the RJ went to the 3ª Vara Empresarial do Rio (Seu Dinheiro, Money Times, InvestNews, all 15 Jul 2026). The RJ was closed on 9 September 2026 (InfoMoney and Ativo Virtual, 12 Nov 2026, citing the 3T26 release). 3T26: net revenue R$3.426bn (+2.4%), reported EBITDA R$276m (-44.2%), net loss R$56m vs R$33m profit in 3T25, net debt R$5.9bn (-14.7%), R$2.2bn of debt converted into shares at face value (InfoMoney, 12 Nov 2026).

**The case for dropping.**
1. *The gate fails arithmetically.* Net debt R$5.9bn needs LTM EBITDA of R$2.36bn to reach 2.5x. 3T26 EBITDA annualised is R$1.1bn (5.3x); even on the 1T26 run-rate of R$494m quoted in W37 (about R$2.0bn a year) it is 3.0x. LTM EBITDA was not retrieved [UNVERIFIED], but no plausible figure clears the gate. The screen admits post-reorg equities "only below 2.5x at emergence and the memo shows it"; the memo cannot show it.
2. *The first clean quarter went backwards.* Lens 1 looks for "a first clean reporting cycle"; Light's was EBITDA -44% and a loss. The cash-margin story (consolidated gross cash margin R$1.0bn, +7.4%; distribution +5.6% on 4.2% market growth, a 1.28% Parcela B adjustment in March 2026 and data-centre demand; generation +14% on GSF and PLD) is real but is the line above the losses, PECLD and opex that make a Rio distributor a Rio distributor.
3. *The headline issue price is not the price.* R$6.29 plus two shares at R$0.01 is about R$2.10 per share for the subscribers; the stock closed at R$2.77 on 17 Nov after 3.67 on 23 Oct (-24.5%, or -34.1% in USD). Post-capitalisation share count after the R$2.2bn conversion [UNVERIFIED]; on 611m shares plus 477m bonus shares the market capitalisation is around R$3bn against R$5.9bn of net debt, so the equity is still the thin slice of an enterprise value that is mostly debt. That is the leverage factor the mandate excludes, in its purest form.
4. *The overhang is dated and large.* 15% of the bonus shares unlock in January 2027; that is the creditor-turned-shareholder selling the screen warns about, with a calendar.
5. *The regulatory piece is still a consultation.* The 2056 concession "recognises" areas of severe operational restriction; how ANEEL compensates for them was open in May and nothing retrieved this session closes it [UNVERIFIED: ANEEL rule status].

**The case for keeping it.** The RJ is over, R$3.7bn of equity came in, net debt fell 14.7% and the company is back in the credit market (an Atlas Público headline cites R$1bn of notas comerciais; the page could not be opened, so the terms are [UNVERIFIED]). If FY2026 shows net debt/EBITDA below 2.5x on the company's own covenant definition, the gate is cleared and lens 1 should look again at that date's price.

**Base rate.** Lens 1 rows are all [UNVERIFIED] in literature.md: no base rate available.

**Verdict: drop from the gated watchlist.** Logged in rejected.csv with the reconsider trigger: a company release showing net debt/EBITDA at or below 2.5x, plus a published ANEEL rule for the restricted areas. The feed keeps marking LIGT3.
