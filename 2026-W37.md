### Candidate 1: EYDAP, Athens Water Supply and Sewerage (EYDAP:ATSE)

**The business.** The regulated water and sewerage monopoly for Athens and the Attica region; served population [UNVERIFIED]. Greek state controls it (50%+1 directly, 11.33% via HRADF, per company statement of Feb 2026).

**Event.** A first-ever regulatory period. Tariffs had been frozen since 2008. RAAEY approved EYDAP's revenue cap and pricing policy through 2029 after consultation, with new tariffs effective 1 January 2026 (company statement, Feb 2026; Athens Times, Dec 2025). Management stated at the FY2025 results (30 Apr 2026) that the framework adds on average €78.3m per year of revenue versus 2024 through 2029, and that roughly €42m of 2025 regulatory revenue is deferred into 2026 to 2029 (ot.gr, 30 Apr 2026).

**Why the price may be wrong.** Reported FY2025 numbers were ugly: revenue €364.7m, EBITDA €32.1m, net loss €21m, with a €15m one-off tax charge for 2019 to 2024 (ot.gr, 30 Apr 2026). Management's adjusted figures were revenue €406.6m, EBITDA €78.6m, net profit €36.1m. The dividend was cut to €0.07 (Simply Wall St, Jun 2026). A screener sees a loss-making utility that cut its dividend. The filings show a business whose allowed revenue has just stepped up by roughly a fifth and whose 2025 P&L does not yet contain any of it. That is the classic lens-5 pattern.

**Path and date.** H1 2026 results are the first reporting period under the new tariffs. Greek issuers must publish H1 by 30 September, so the catalyst date is **30 Sep 2026**, with FY2026 results (late April 2027) as the confirmation. Slip scenario: results land on time by law; the slip risk is in content (regulatory revenue booked below the €78m run rate), not timing.

**Under-covered?** Analyst count [UNVERIFIED]; market cap around €1.3bn at €12.28 (Yahoo, 4 Sep 2026) on 106.5m shares [UNVERIFIED share count]. Not in MSCI Greece standard [UNVERIFIED]. Meets the tiebreaker if fewer than five analysts cover it; the user should check on the IBKR research tab.

**Gates.** Positive operating cash flow in two of three years [UNVERIFIED; needs the annual reports]. Net cash: total cash €276m at last balance sheet (Yahoo, mrq) against debt [UNVERIFIED], so the 2.5x leverage gate is almost certainly cleared. Auditor [UNVERIFIED]. English filings: yes. IBKR access to Athens Exchange: [UNVERIFIED, likely yes]. ADV in USD terms: [UNVERIFIED]; must exceed US$150k.

**What would have to be true.** (1) The H1 2026 accounts show revenue recognised at or near the approved cap, not deferred again. (2) The €2.5bn ten-year capex plan is funded mostly by EU structural funds (56% of 2025 capex came from ESPA; 37% of the €2.5bn already approved per management) and the €250m EIB line, so the net cash does not simply evaporate. (3) The state as controller does not reopen tariffs before 2029. The Athens mayor publicly attacked the increase in February 2026; the company answered that the state receives most of the dividends. That is the politics of it in one exchange.

**Own target, arithmetic shown.** Run-rate EBITDA under the framework: adjusted 2025 EBITDA €78.6m plus roughly €70m of the €78.3m annual revenue uplift falling to EBITDA (regulated revenue with largely fixed opex) gives about €150m. At 9x EV/EBITDA (mature European water assets; multiple is my assumption, [UNVERIFIED] against peers this week) EV is €1.35bn, plus €276m cash and ignoring debt gives equity of about €1.63bn, or **about €15.3 per share, roughly 25% above €12.28**. If the multiple is 7x the target is €12.5, i.e. no upside. The trade only works if the run-rate EBITDA shows up in the H1 accounts.

**Bear case, at least as long.** The stock is up 57.6% over one year to 4 Sep 2026 (Yahoo), including a 27% surge in July. A good part of the framework is therefore already priced; this is trap 1 in the screen. The €78.3m uplift is management's number from a press briefing, not the regulator's decision text, which this memo did not read. The €323m of prior-period claims management is still pursuing have not been approved by RAAEY; a bull case that leans on them is leaning on air. Drought: Attica's reservoirs were at their worst in 30 years in late 2025; volumes fall when the state imposes restrictions, and a revenue cap protects revenue only if the reconciliation mechanism actually works in year one, which nobody has seen yet. Capex is going from €15m a year to a €2.5bn decade; if EU funding disbursements lag, the net cash turns into net debt and the dividend stays cut. The dividend cut to €0.07 tells you the board is already worried about that. Governance: state-controlled, board reconstituted in April 2026, a July 2026 draft water-sector law under consultation. And the sector reform draft (July 2026) confirms RAAEY pricing but could change cost-recovery rules; a regulator that took 17 years to grant an increase can take one year to claw it back. Finally, the informed seller: Paulson & Co. held about 10% historically; if that stake has been distributed into the July rally, the seller who knows the asset best has been exiting into the re-rating [UNVERIFIED].

**Verdict.** Paper position, logged today. Size: US$1,000 hypothetical (the phase-2 minimum, equal to 8% of a US$12,500 book). Entry €12.28 (4 Sep close, Yahoo; not today's price). Target €15.3. Catalyst 30 Sep 2026. Kill criteria: (a) H1 2026 reported revenue below €200m, meaning the cap is not being booked; (b) any RAAEY or ministerial act reopening the 2026 to 2029 pricing; (c) net debt appearing on the balance sheet before FY2027. Gate date: week 40 (next lens 7/anti-thesis week is week 39, gate at week 40). Strong candidate, buyable at that gate only if the satellite were open, which it is not.

### Candidate 2: Sanepar (SAPR11:B3)

**The business.** State-controlled water and sewerage company for Paraná, listed since 2002, Nível 2 governance, third periodic tariff review completed in 2025 with AGEPAR as regulator (Fitch report on Sanepar IR site, 13 Mar 2026).

**Event.** A regulatory decision, already taken: on 23 June 2026 AGEPAR's board ruled that a roughly R$3.94bn precatório (federal tax refund received 1 Sep 2025) goes 100% to tariff modicity, 50% as bill discounts and 50% as non-remunerated investments, indexed at the regulatory rate (Investidor10, 25 Aug 2026; Gazeta do Paraná, 24 Jun 2026). Market had expected the old 75/25 split, which would have left roughly R$1bn with the company (Investidor10, 18 Apr 2026). Sanepar filed a mandado de segurança in April and cancelled its H1 2026 JCP after the ruling (Seu Dinheiro, 25 Jun 2026).

**Why the price may be wrong.** Units fell around 7% on the March technical note and traded at R$37.21 on the day of the June ruling (Investidor10), against a pre-dispute range near R$41 in April. The market has priced the loss of the R$1bn dividend and, arguably, a discount for a regulator that changed the rules after the cash arrived. The asymmetry: the downside (zero to shareholders) is the base case already in the price; any court outcome restoring the 75/25 split is pure upside; and the 50% "non-onerous investment" leg is capex the company would partly have done anyway, funded by a customer credit rather than the balance sheet, which is not value-neutral for the shareholder even though it is not remunerated.

**Path and date: this is where it fails.** The mechanism is a court ruling in the Vara da Fazenda Pública de Curitiba, with appeals to follow. There is no date. The screen requires one. Sanepar therefore goes to the **watchlist, not a paper position**, until a hearing or judgment date exists. Second, the state election is in October 2026 and the governor's camp has floated privatisation for two years (Folha de Londrina, Jan 2025); the CEO said in April 2026 the company has no mandate to privatise (Money Times). A privatisation announcement would be a dated event; the memo does not assume one.

**Gates.** Brazil cap applies (0 to 10% of portfolio; there is no other Brazilian exposure). Liquidity: SAPR11 is a Nível 2 unit with institutional volume; ADV in USD [UNVERIFIED but almost certainly above US$150k]. Leverage: Fitch AAA(bra) stable; net debt/EBITDA [UNVERIFIED, needs the 2T26 release]. Starboard conflict: the user must confirm none.

**Bear case.** A state-owned utility litigating against its own regulator in an election year is a governance red flag, not a catalyst. The 2025 tariff review is done, so the next reset is years away; an unhappy regulator has other levers before then (quality penalties, RAB glosas). Precatório funds that go to bill discounts reduce revenue in the years they are applied, which the market may not yet have fully modelled. The person on the other side of this trade is a local institution that read the AGEPAR technical note in March and sold; that seller is informed, not forced. If Sanepar wins in court the state may simply direct the company to distribute less, as it already did with the JCP.

**Verdict.** Watchlist. Log price R$37.21 (25 Aug 2026 intraday, Investidor10). What changes it: a scheduled judgment date, or a fato relevante on privatisation with a legislative timetable.

### Candidate 3: Light S.A. (LIGT3:B3), gated

**The business.** Rio de Janeiro electricity distributor since 1904, in recuperação judicial since May 2023 with around R$11bn of pre-petition debt (Mais Retorno, May 2023). Concession renewed for 30 years to 4 June 2056 by the 8th amendment signed 8 May 2026, with new contract language recognising areas of severe operational restriction (theft, non-technical losses, violence) as distinct (Monitor Mercantil, 9 May 2026; Investidor10, 11 May 2026).

**Event.** Two dated-ish events: the capitalisation of R$3.2bn to R$3.7bn via capital increase and credit conversion, and the court order closing the RJ, both guided by management for 2026 (ADVFN, 11 May 2026). Management targets net debt/EBITDA below 2x after capitalisation.

**Why it cannot be logged today.** (1) Leverage gate: pro forma net debt was R$6.7bn at 1T26 (Arevista, 20 May 2026) against a quarterly EBITDA of R$494m (Investidor10), i.e. above 3x annualised. The screen admits post-reorg equities only where the emergence balance sheet shows below 2.5x. That balance sheet does not exist yet. (2) Starboard conflict unconfirmed. (3) Price data stale: R$4.20 on 8 May 2026 (ADVFN).

**What would make it a candidate.** The homologatory decision closing the RJ plus the first post-capitalisation balance sheet with net debt/EBITDA below 2.5x, and a written confirmation from the user that Starboard is not active or restricted. If all three arrive, this is the purest lens-1-meets-lens-5 situation on B3 and would be logged at that date's price, not today's.

**Bear case, briefly, because it is not yet a candidate.** Rio non-technical losses are structural; the new contract "recognises" them without yet defining compensation ("how this will happen is still being debated", CEO to Brazil Journal via Investidor10). A Tanure-linked vehicle sold 18.6m shares (Investidor10), which is the creditor-turned-shareholder selling the screen warns about. Aneel's regulatory treatment of restricted areas is a consultation, not a rule. R$10bn of capex over five years on a distributor that just emerged from RJ is a financing plan that has not been tested by the credit market.

**Verdict.** Watchlist, gated. Not a paper position.
